# Editorial Policy

GameIntel treats structured source records, claims, evidence, and provenance as
the canonical data. Articles are reviewed output projections of that data, not
the only supported output.

AI assists with research, extraction and drafting. It cannot approve sources,
verify claims, approve articles or publish. A local human operator must review
the source evidence, editorial revision and final publication decision.

We prefer official sources and independently reproducible evidence. Reposts
from the same upstream material count as one provenance lineage. Uncertainty,
contradictions and missing conditions remain visible to the operator.

Published facts carry their own evidence level rather than inheriting a confidence
score from the article. Use `confirmed` for human-reviewed authoritative evidence,
`corroborated` for independent supporting lineages, `suspected` for insufficiently
verified reporting, and `disputed` when credible evidence conflicts. Community and
leak-related facts must use explicit attribution language. Leaked assets and direct
leak URLs are never public citations.
